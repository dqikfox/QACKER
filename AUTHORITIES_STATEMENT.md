## Purpose

QACKER is a security-research and investigation-support dashboard. It is designed to help authorized analysts correlate technical evidence (network indicators, tool output, media metadata, direction-finding bearings) into structured, hashed, and exportable records.

It does **not** perform active collection, interception, exploitation, or person tracking. It processes only evidence that an authorized analyst supplies.

## Lawful-use constraints

- Every case requires an explicit authorization declaration and basis before it can be created.
- The system does not scan, probe, geolocate, intercept, or exploit third-party systems.
- Media metadata extraction (ExifTool) operates only on local files supplied by the analyst.
- Bearing triangulation and map visualisation operate only on analyst-supplied station positions and bearings, plus the operator’s own browser location when explicitly enabled.
- Technical correlation scores are uncalibrated heuristics for prioritisation. They are not probabilities of identity, guilt, or location.
- The project is not intended for, and must not be used for, vigilantism, entrapment, unauthorized surveillance, or any unlawful activity.

## Intended use if encountered by authorities

If this repository or any derived system is encountered by law enforcement, child-protection agencies, or other competent authorities:

1. It is offered as a potential technical aid for **lawful, authorized investigations** only.
2. Any evidence it produces is analyst-supplied and hashed at ingestion. Chain-of-custody and legal process remain the responsibility of the investigating authority.
3. The author is available to explain the design, limitations, and intended use if contacted through appropriate official channels.
4. The project does not claim to have produced any successful real-world apprehension.
5. All use must comply with applicable law in the relevant jurisdiction.
